Privacy Policy for SquadLive

Effective Date: July 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored, and shared when you use the SquadLive mobile application and its related services (collectively, the "Service").

1. Information We Collect

SquadLive does not require you to create an account or provide your name or email address to use its core features. Depending on how you use the Service, we may process the following information:

  • App and device information: Device identifiers, operating system information, app version, language, general usage events, and diagnostic information.
  • Profile and preference information: Your selected profile details, avatar, app settings, virtual coin balance, VIP status, saved session information, and reward history.
  • AI interaction information: Text generated from what you say or type during an AI interaction, along with settings needed to generate an appropriate response.
  • Reward submission information: Post links or screenshots that you voluntarily submit for share-reward review.
  • Purchase information: Product identifiers, transaction identifiers, subscription status, and related purchase records when in-app purchases are enabled.

2. Camera, Microphone, Photos, and Speech Recognition

SquadLive may request access to your camera, microphone, photo library, and Apple's speech recognition service to provide the simulated live-room experience, understand spoken input, let you choose an avatar or reward screenshot, and save content when you request it.

Camera and microphone access require your permission. SquadLive does not continuously upload your raw camera feed or raw microphone recording to our servers. Speech-derived text may be sent for AI response generation as described below.

3. AI Services

When you use AI reply features, text from your interaction and limited session context may be sent to the SquadLive backend and then to the configured third-party AI provider, currently DeepSeek, to generate a response. Do not submit sensitive personal, medical, financial, legal, or confidential information through AI interactions.

AI-generated responses are provided for companionship and entertainment. They are not medical care, therapy, crisis support, or professional advice.

4. Firebase Analytics

SquadLive uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand general app usage, such as app launches and feature engagement. Firebase may automatically process device and app information in accordance with Google's policies. We use this information to maintain and improve the Service.

For more information, please review the Google Privacy Policy and Firebase Privacy and Security information.

5. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Provide AI replies and simulated live-room features.
  • Save app preferences, virtual items, rewards, and account state.
  • Process and verify eligible in-app purchases and subscriptions.
  • Review voluntary share-reward submissions.
  • Monitor reliability, prevent abuse, and improve the Service.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

6. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited information with service providers that help operate the Service, including cloud hosting providers, AI providers, Google Firebase, and Apple for in-app purchases. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect users or the Service, or in connection with a business transfer.

7. Data Storage and Retention

Some profile and preference information is stored locally on your device. Information sent to the backend may be stored on our hosting infrastructure for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Service, maintain balances and reward records, resolve support issues, prevent abuse, and meet legal obligations.

You may remove locally stored app information by deleting the app. To request deletion of information stored by the SquadLive backend, contact us using the address below and provide enough information for us to identify the relevant app record.

8. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information. However, no electronic transmission or storage method is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children's Privacy

SquadLive is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Service, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

10. International Processing

Your information may be processed in countries other than the country where you live. Data protection laws in those countries may differ from those in your jurisdiction.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the revised policy on this page and update the effective date when changes become effective.
